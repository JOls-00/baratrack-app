1. Who we are

BaraTrack is operated by [Barangay name, city, province] ("the Barangay"), the personal information controller. Contact for privacy concerns (Data Protection Officer): [name, email, phone, office address].

2. What we collect and why

  • Account details: name, username, email, password (stored only as a hash), contact number, address, birth date, ID number, profile photo. Purpose: to create and verify your resident account.
  • Complaint and case records: details of incidents, names of complainants and respondents, hearing schedules, evidence files you or barangay staff upload (photos, videos, documents). Purpose: to receive, process and resolve barangay complaints.
  • Location: the incident location you choose, or your device location if you allow it. Purpose: to record where an incident happened and show it on the incident map. Location is only read when you use the location picker.
  • Documents issued to you by the Barangay (e.g. certificates).
  • Technical data: IP address in security and rate-limit logs.

3. Legal basis

Processing is based on your consent and on the Barangay's functions under the Local Government Code and Katarungang Pambarangay law, consistent with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). [Confirm with counsel.]

4. Who can see your data

Barangay administrators and staff authorised by the Barangay. Residents can only see their own complaints, and cases they are a party to. We do not sell personal data.
